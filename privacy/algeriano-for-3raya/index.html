4. البريد والأمان — نقاط يجب أن تعرفها

يستخدم التطبيق خادم Gmail (smtp.gmail.com على المنفذ 587 مع STARTTLS). التشفير هنا أثناء النقل فقط وليس تشفيرًا تامًا من الطرف إلى الطرف، ومستلم الرسالة يرى الصورة والموقع كما يرى أي بريد إلكتروني.

إفصاح صريح: في هذا الإصدار تُحفظ بيانات Gmail (العنوان وكلمة مرور التطبيقات) في مساحة التطبيق الخاصة غير مشفّرة، والتطبيق مسموح بنسخه احتياطيًا عبر Android Auto Backup، ما يعني أن نسخة Google One الاحتياطية قد تحتوي هذه القيم. استخدم كلمة مرور تطبيقات مخصّصة وليس كلمة مرور حسابك، واحذفها من داخل التطبيق قبل بيع الهاتف أو تسليمه أو نقله، ثم ألغِها من حساب Google. الإصدار التالي سينقل هذه القيم إلى تخزين مشفّر ويمنع نسخها احتياطيًا.

1. Scope

This policy explains how Algeriano for 3raya handles information when it is used to protect the phone of its owner against wrong unlock attempts. The app is built to run on a device you own or are explicitly authorised to protect. It is not designed to monitor, track or photograph other people secretly, or to access devices without their knowledge and consent.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking identifiers, no developer server, no user account, no dashboard. Nothing is uploaded to us, and we cannot read your mailbox or your photos.

3. Permissions and why

  • Camera: capture one photo on a failed unlock attempt after you enable protection.
  • Location (precise/coarse): attach the phone's position as a map link to the alert.
  • Notifications + foreground service (camera/location): complete the capture and send while the event happens.
  • Device Admin: to observe wrong-PIN attempts only. The app contains no remote lock and no remote wipe. You can revoke this in Android settings at any time.
  • Start at boot, wake lock, ignore battery optimisation: keep protection alive after a restart.
  • Internet: send the alert through your Gmail and open the map link.

The app does not request SMS permissions, contacts, your files or photos, or the list of installed apps.

4. Email and security — what you should know

The app talks to Gmail (smtp.gmail.com, port 587, STARTTLS). That encryption covers transport only — it is not end-to-end encryption, and the recipient of the alert sees the photo and location like any normal email.

Full disclosure: in this version the Gmail address and App Password are stored in the app's private storage unencrypted, and the app is currently eligible for Android Auto Backup, which means a Google One backup may contain those values. Use a dedicated App Password, never your account password, and delete the credentials inside the app before selling, handing over or resetting the phone, then revoke that App Password in your Google account. A future version will move them to keystore-encrypted storage and exclude them from backup.

5. Third parties

  • Google Gmail / SMTP: carries the alert message; governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps (link): opens in your browser or maps app when you tap it; Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Google Play Services (location): Android's location provider.
  • Firebase libraries are present in the app package but are not configured and not initialised (the app ships no Firebase configuration file), so no data is sent to Firebase or Google Analytics.
  • No ad networks, no tracking SDKs, no data sold or shared with brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Photos and logs stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Clear storage), or uninstall it. Alert emails stay in your own mailbox, which you control. We keep no copy of any of it. To request deletion of anything that could have reached us — which by design is nothing — email the address above.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. Do not use it to watch a child or another person's device without the required consent and supervision.
